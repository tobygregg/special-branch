What we do

From a single limb to a whole tree lifted out by crane. Pick a service to see what’s involved.

Felling & dismantling

Where there’s room, a tree can come down in one. Where there isn’t, we climb it and take it down in sections, planning every cut around your house, walls and garden.

What’s included

  • Sectional dismantling by trained climbers
  • Controlled felling where space allows
  • Rigging and lowering over roofs and walls
  • Brash and timber cleared away
Ask about felling

Crane-assisted removals

For big trees on tight sites we work alongside a crane, so large sections are lifted straight out instead of being lowered through the garden. Quicker, and safer for everything underneath.

What’s included

  • Large trees over houses, walls and lanes
  • Big sections lifted out in one piece
  • Timber loaded straight onto the lorry
  • Machinery lifted in where access is tight
Ask about crane work

Storm damage & fallen trees

After a storm we clear fallen and split trees, make damaged ones safe and take the debris away. We worked through the clear-ups after Storm Ciarán and Storm Goretti.

What’s included

  • Fallen trees cleared from gardens, drives and lanes
  • Hanging and split limbs made safe
  • Damaged trees reduced or taken down
  • Every bit of debris removed
Report storm damage

Pruning & crown work

Reducing, thinning or lifting a crown lets in light, takes weight off long limbs and keeps a tree a sensible size, without losing it.

What’s included

  • Crown reduction and thinning
  • Crown lifting over drives, paths and roofs
  • Dead and damaged limbs removed
  • Trees cut back from buildings and boundaries
Ask about pruning

Stump & root removal

Once the tree is down, the stump and root plate can come out too, so the ground is ready for replanting, a new lawn or building work.

What’s included

  • Stumps and root plates lifted out
  • Roots and spoil taken away
  • Ground left ready for what comes next
Ask about stumps

Logs & clear-up

Every job finishes with a proper clear-up. Brash, timber and debris are taken away, or the wood is cut into logs and stacked if you’d like to keep it.

What’s included

  • Brash and debris removed from site
  • Large timber loaded and taken away
  • Logs cut and stacked if you want them
  • Lawns, paths and drives left tidy
Ask about clear-up

How a job runs

What happens from your first call to the last load leaving.

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Call or send a message telling us about the tree and where it is.

  2. 2

    We take a look

    We look at the tree, the access and what’s around it, then explain what we’d do and give you a price.

  3. 3

    The work

    The team arrives on the agreed day with the climbers, rigging and lifting gear the job needs.

  4. 4

    Clear-up

    Debris goes with us and the site is left tidy. Logs stay, stacked, if you want them.
